Truth News Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 15, 2026

Truth News (“Truth News”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) is a source-first news reading application. This Privacy Policy explains what information the Android application collects, how it is used, and the choices available to users.

1. Information We Collect

Truth News does not require an account, sign-up, password, or authentication. We do not ask users to provide their name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or other profile information in the app.

The app may store limited app data locally on the device, such as preferences or cached information needed for the app to function. This local data is not intended to identify you personally.

2. News and Network Requests

The app connects to our news backend to retrieve current news stories. These requests contain the information necessary to provide the requested news service, such as selected news categories.

The backend retrieves publicly available RSS news feeds from third-party news sources and returns article information to the app. The app may open an article's original URL in your browser when you choose to read the source article.

3. Server and Technical Information

When you connect to an internet service, standard network infrastructure may process technical information such as an IP address, request time, device or browser information, and security-related logs. Truth News does not use this information to create user profiles or for targeted advertising.

Our backend does not require user accounts and is designed to provide news data without collecting a user profile.

4. Third-Party Websites and News Sources

Truth News links to articles hosted by third-party news organizations. When you open a source article, you leave Truth News and are subject to that third party's privacy policy and terms. We do not control the privacy practices of third-party websites.

5. Advertising and Sale of Personal Information

Truth News does not sell personal information. The app does not use personal information for targeted advertising.

6. Permissions

The Android application requires internet access to retrieve news. It does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, precise location, or files for its core news-reading functionality.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the service. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

Truth News is a general news application and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app does not require an account or ask for personal profile information, it is designed to minimize collection of personal information.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Because Truth News does not require user accounts or maintain user profiles, there is generally no personal account data for us to delete. Locally stored app data can be removed by uninstalling the app or clearing its app data through Android settings.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app or its data practices change. The updated policy will be published at this URL with a revised effective date.
